## Alternatives considered

Keep total physical object movement in every skill: source-faithful but mathematically rejects intentional LIFT/transport at 0.01 m, even with perfect calibration. Scalar registration alone cannot fix it.

Use observed same-pixel depth speed, zero from equal frames, or divide motion by a shorter interval: easier software but does not establish the required target/horizon source and is rejected.

Recommended: explicit skill/reference semantics with genuine source-bound fixed endpoints for intentional transport, independent geometry/terminal tracking calibration and mutable/contact certificates where needed. The threshold/horizon remain unchanged, and each changed quantity has a visible versioned meaning for root review.
