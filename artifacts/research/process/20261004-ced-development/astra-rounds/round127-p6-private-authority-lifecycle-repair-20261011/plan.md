Preserve exact per-response prior hard-open bound through consumption. A valid new segment cannot rescue a response consumed at/after its prior deadline.
5. **Evidence gaps E6/E7 confirmed:** old confirmation_lost manually invalidates before synchronous confirm; old restart calls a completely finished _exercise and compares unchanged attempts. Correct both. They are source-level test deficiencies, not observed failing executions. Scope proof decorator/string coverage cannot establish G1 safety.

A supported normal RGBD/VISION_CLOSED_LOOP queued draft with runtime_delay_ms>0 can reach it. Existing callback repository call is not producer evidence.
